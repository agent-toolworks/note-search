# 2026-10-12: 504s on /pay

## What happened

From 18:05 to 19:40 UTC about 30% of `POST /pay` requests returned 504 Gateway
Timeout. Card attempts piled up in `payment_attempts`.

## Cause

Immediate retries in orders-api multiplied traffic to payment-gateway when the
card processor slowed down, and the gateway's connection pool ran out.

## Conclusion

Backoff with jitter on the client fixed it; a bigger pool only moved the limit.
