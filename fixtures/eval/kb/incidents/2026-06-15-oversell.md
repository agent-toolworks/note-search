# 2026-06-15: items sold beyond stock

## What happened

Sold-out items could still be bought for about an hour; 212 orders had no stock.

## Cause

The inventory-sweeper host's clock ran 20 minutes fast, so it released
reservations before they expired.

## Conclusion

The sweeper uses database time. Hosts alert on clock drift over 2 seconds.
